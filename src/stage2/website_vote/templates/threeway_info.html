Three-Way Analysis Comparison

Compare AI contract analysis approaches

Welcome! This is an comparison study that evaluates three different AI approaches for contract analysis. Your expertise will help determine which methods produce the most useful and accurate results.

Your Task:

  • Review the contract summary and, if necessary, the full contract document
  • Compare three AI-generated analyses side by side
  • Select the best overall analysis
  • Rate each analysis on four key dimensions (1-5 scale)

Rating Dimensions

  • Clarity: How clear and understandable is the analysis?
  • Legal Soundness: How legally accurate and reliable?
  • Reasoning Depth: How thorough and detailed?
  • Human Alignment: How well does it match what a human lawyer would focus on?

Instructions:

Focus on which analysis would be most useful to a legal practitioner. Consider accuracy, clarity, and practical value. Each analysis will be rated independently, then you'll select the overall winner.

All data is collected anonymously for academic research. Your thoughtful evaluations are essential for advancing AI-powered legal analysis tools. You can do as many evaluations as you want and you can stop at any time.
